Accepted one product group / 20 colors, 1 kg × 1.75 mm only. The [official product page](https://www.sunlu.com/products/PETG2.0) supplies density 1.22 g/cm³, HEX values, matte finish and temperature ranges; the [official store](https://store.sunlu.com/products/petg-v2-3d-printer-filament-1kg) binds the sold package. The wider diameter/weight buttons on the marketing page are not a license to generate additional combinations.

The upstream contributor reports directly weighing the PETG 2.0 Gen3 empty spool at 189 g. This is community measurement evidence, not independent laboratory verification. No Gen3 tare was propagated to older Sunlu products or other families. The 250°C nozzle starting value is the contributor's tested value within the manufacturer's range, not a separate manufacturer recommendation.

Before insertion, compared all existing Sunlu groups and expanded names for `2.0`, `v2` and normalized PETG line names. Existing PLA+ 2.0 is a different material/product and was preserved, including its identifiers. Existing PETG and High Speed Matte PETG were preserved.
